# Demo script (about 3 minutes)

Start from a fresh sample archive: `npm run seed -- --force` (let the scene art finish, or run `npm run art` beforehand), then `npm run dev`. Unlock with `kinjal-demo`. Use Chrome so live transcription works, but keep the typed lines below ready in case the room is loud. Turn sound on, with headphones if you can: the score is in 3D.

Everything in the sample is fictional. Say so if anyone asks.

## 1. Hook (15 s)

Open the Life Path.

> If you start journaling at 23, you're starting at chapter 23. The Living Draft lets you fill in the earlier chapters, in any order.

Scroll down the path. Point at the dim years above for the future, the dashed rings on estimated memories, and the empty years that are still open. Turn a page with the section tabs on the right to show it's a notebook.

## 1b. Step back in (30 s)

Tap August 17, 2024. Three memories light up as a constellation. Tap "Moving day with Mom": the scene, score and weather all change as you dive in. Tap the "Mom's promise" thread to jump twelve years back, then press Escape to pull out.

## 2. Capture (30 s)

Press Capture and talk, or type:

> Dad sided with Mom and nobody took me anywhere that summer. I was so mad at both of them.

The words appear while you talk and the core pulses with your voice. Press "Done, find its place".

## 3. Placement and match (20 s)

On the review screen:

- The tidy-ups are struck through. The original words are kept.
- It found the existing memory "Mom's promise" and says why: shared words, same people, same season.
- Dad and Mom are linked to the people who already exist, with their roles.

Choose "Add to this memory", "It adds details", then save. "Memory updated, telling 2".

## 4. Evolution (40 s)

Press Capture again on "Mom's promise" ("Tell more") and say:

> Thinking about that summer again, I'm not sure Mom actually promised. Maybe I just assumed we'd go somewhere.

Save it as an addition, then press "See what changed". The prompt reads: "Earlier you remembered … now you remember … These details seem different. How would you like to preserve them?" Pick "Mark this detail as uncertain". Show the side-by-side view and the timeline of tellings.

The recital memory already has an open difference ("I forgot the middle section" against "I didn't forget the middle section") if you'd rather show that one.

## 5. People and the map (25 s)

Tap "Mom" in the text. Filter her timeline to Central. Then open the Memory Map and click the full-circle thread between "The lunch table" (eating alone at 8 or 9) and "Lunch with Priya" (sitting with the new girl at 16). The panel quotes both memories.

## 6. Ask My Story (20 s)

Ask "How has my relationship with Mom changed over time?" Point at the citations, the line saying this is how you remember Mom and not who she is, and the gaps it admits to.

## 7. Under the hood (20 s)

Open Settings, then "See how it works". Show the three boxes, the pipeline and the live evaluation numbers, including the misses. Mention that it runs entirely on the laptop, costs nothing, and that only live transcription in Chrome leaves the machine.

## If something goes wrong

- No microphone or a noisy room: type. The flow is identical.
- The match isn't suggested: choose the memory manually from "Keep it as a new memory" → go back, or use Tell more from the memory page.
- Anything stuck: refresh. Nothing is lost; every write is already saved.
